About Campus Assets

Campus Assets is a college-focused asset and equipment management system built to simplify the way institutions track, allocate, maintain, and analyze their physical resources.

Why Campus Assets?

Managing college equipment through spreadsheets or manual records can make it difficult to track availability, assignments, maintenance history, and overall asset usage. Campus Assets provides a centralized digital solution designed to make these processes more organized, transparent, and efficient.

Centralized Inventory

Store and manage important information about equipment and other institutional assets.

Controlled Allocations

Allocate assets safely while automatically protecting inventory quantities.

Maintenance Visibility

Track maintenance records, costs, technicians, and equipment status.

Actionable Insights

Use charts, reports, filters, and exports to understand asset usage and inventory conditions.

Built With Modern Technologies

Python Django MySQL Bootstrap 5 JavaScript Chart.js openpyxl ReportLab

Designed for Simplicity and Control

Campus Assets combines essential asset management workflows into a single, easy-to-use platform while maintaining data consistency, responsive design, and practical reporting tools.
