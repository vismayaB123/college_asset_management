Help & User Guide

Learn how to use Campus Assets to manage equipment, allocations, maintenance, notifications, settings, and reports.

1 Managing Assets

  1. Navigate to the Assets section.
  2. Click Add Asset.
  3. Enter the asset code, name, category, quantity, location, condition, status, and other available information.
  4. Save the asset.
  5. Use search and filters to quickly locate assets.
The system protects inventory integrity by ensuring available quantities cannot exceed total quantities.

2 Allocating Assets

  1. Navigate to Allocations.
  2. Select an available asset.
  3. Choose the department.
  4. Enter the assigned person and quantity.
  5. Set allocation and expected return dates.
  6. Save the allocation.
Assets cannot be allocated beyond their available quantity.

3 Returning Allocated Assets

When an allocated asset is returned, use the Return action in the Allocations section. The system automatically updates the allocation status, records the return date, and restores the allocated quantity back to the asset's available inventory.

4 Managing Maintenance

  1. Navigate to Maintenance.
  2. Add a maintenance record.
  3. Select the asset.
  4. Enter the maintenance description, date, technician, cost, and status.
  5. Update the record as maintenance progresses.
Assets with active Pending or In Progress maintenance records are visually identified as being under maintenance.

5 Understanding Notifications

Low Stock:

Alerts you when available asset quantities fall at or below the configured low stock threshold.

Overdue Allocations:

Alerts you when an asset has passed its expected return date and has not been returned.

Active Maintenance:

Alerts you when maintenance records are Pending or In Progress.

Notification preferences can be managed from the Settings page.

6 Settings and Preferences

Profile:
Update your personal information and profile picture.
Security:
Change your account password securely.
Notifications:
Choose which notification types you want to receive.
Appearance:
Choose between Light, Dark, or System appearance preferences.
System Preferences:
Administrators can manage global settings such as the low stock threshold.

7 Reports and Analytics

The Reports page provides summary metrics, interactive charts, detailed tables, and filters.

CSV

Useful for simple data viewing and spreadsheet applications.

Excel

Provides a formatted spreadsheet suitable for further analysis.

PDF

Provides a printable report format.

Exports automatically respect the filters currently applied to the report.
